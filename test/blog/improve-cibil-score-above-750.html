5 Actionable Steps to Improve Your CIBIL Score Above 750

Anitha Selvaraj
Senior Home Loan Specialist, Greenvill Associates | Published: August 2026

In the modern Indian banking landscape, your 3-digit CIBIL Credit Score (ranging from 300 to 900) functions as your financial passport. Major banks like SBI, HDFC, ICICI, and Axis offer special discounted interest rates and zero processing fee waivers exclusively to applicants possessing a CIBIL score of 750 or higher.

1. Pay All Credit Card Bills & EMIs Before Due Date

Payment history accounts for approximately 35% of your total CIBIL score. Even a single 30-day Days Past Due (DPD) record can pull your score down by 50 to 80 points. Set up auto-debit standing instructions on your primary savings account to ensure zero missed due dates.

2. Keep Credit Card Utilization Below 30%

Credit Utilization Ratio (CUR) is the percentage of your available credit card limit being used. For example, if your credit card limit is ₹1,00,000, try to keep your monthly outstanding balance below ₹30,000. High utilization signals credit hunger to underwriters.

3. Maintain a Healthy Credit Mix

Having a balanced mix of secured credit (such as Home Loans or Auto Loans) and unsecured credit (such as Credit Cards or Personal Loans) demonstrates credit maturity. Avoid carrying multiple active instant unsecured app loans simultaneously.

4. Avoid Applying to Multiple Banks Simultaneously

Every time you submit a loan application directly to a bank, the lender initiates a Hard Inquiry on your credit file. Multiple hard inquiries within a short 30-day window penalize your score. Working with an authorized DSA like Greenvill avoids unnecessary hard inquiries because we pre-verify your eligibility before filing.

5. Regularly Audit Your Official CIBIL Report for Errors

Nearly 15% of CIBIL reports contain clerical errors, such as a closed loan still shown as active or an incorrect DPD record caused by bank server delays. Dispute any erroneous entries online via the CIBIL dispute portal immediately.
