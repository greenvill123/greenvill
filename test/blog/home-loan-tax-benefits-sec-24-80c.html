Maximizing Tax Benefits Under Sec 24 & 80C For Home Buyers

Senthil Nathan
Head of Commercial Credit, Greenvill Associates | Published: August 2026

Purchasing a residential house property is not only a milestone personal achievement, but also one of the most effective tax planning instruments under the Indian Income Tax Act. By utilizing provisions under Section 24(b), Section 80C, and Section 80EEA, home buyers in Trichy can significantly reduce their taxable income every financial year.

1. Section 24(b): Deduction on Interest Paid (Up to ₹2 Lakhs)

Under Section 24(b), individual taxpayers can claim a tax deduction of up to ₹2,00,000 per financial year on the interest portion of their home loan EMI for a self-occupied residential property. If the property is rented out, there is no upper cap on interest deduction against rental income.

2. Section 80C: Deduction on Principal Repayment (Up to ₹1.5 Lakhs)

The principal portion of your home loan EMI paid during the financial year qualifies for tax exemption under Section 80C up to a maximum limit of ₹1,50,000. Additionally, stamp duty and registration fees paid during the house purchase year can also be claimed under 80C within the overall limit.

3. Double Your Tax Exemption with Joint Home Loans!

If you apply for a home loan jointly with your spouse or co-borrower (who is also a co-owner of the property), BOTH co-borrowers can individually claim up to ₹2 Lakhs under Sec 24(b) and ₹1.5 Lakhs under Sec 80C. This effectively doubles your family's total tax deduction to ₹7,00,000 annually!

4. How to Obtain Your Home Loan Interest Certificate

To claim these tax deductions with your employer or during IT return filing, simply download the annual Provisional Interest Certificate issued by your lending bank at the start of the financial year.
