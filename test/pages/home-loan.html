Eligibility Criteria

Home Loan Eligibility Guidelines

Requirements for Salaried Individuals and Self-Employed Business Owners.

Salaried Individuals

  • Age: 21 to 65 Years at loan maturity
  • Min Net Monthly Salary: ₹25,000 / month
  • Work Experience: Min 2 Years total experience
  • CIBIL Score: 700+ for best interest rate tiers

Self-Employed Business Owners

  • Age: 23 to 70 Years at loan maturity
  • Business Continuity: Min 3 Years registered operations
  • Min Annual Income (ITR): ₹3.5 Lakhs net annual income
  • CIBIL Score: 700+ preferred (Banking surrogate available)
Documentation

Required Documents For Home Loan

Keep these documents ready for doorstep collection by our Trichy executive team.

  • ✓ KYC Proof: Aadhaar Card, PAN Card, Voter ID / Passport
  • ✓ Income Proof (Salaried): Last 3 Months Salary Slips & Form 16
  • ✓ Income Proof (Business): Last 3 Years Audited ITR & Profit & Loss Statements
  • ✓ Banking Proof: Last 6 Months Main Operating Bank Account Statement
  • ✓ Property Documents: Sale Deed, Parent Title Deeds, Approved Plan Layout
  • ✓ Verification: Encumbrance Certificate (EC) for 30 Years & Property Tax Receipt
