Eligibility Criteria

Business Loan Eligibility Requirements

Proprietorship & Partnership Firms

  • Business Vintage: Min 3 Years registered operational history
  • Min Annual Turnover: ₹25 Lakhs per annum
  • Financial Track Record: Positive Net Worth & Profitability for 2 Years
  • Promoter CIBIL: 700+

Private Limited & LLPs

  • Business Vintage: Min 2 Years incorporated
  • Audited Financials: Last 2 Years audited ITR & balance sheet
  • Banking Track Record: 12 Months main current account statement
  • Director CIBIL: 700+
Documentation

Required Documents For Business Loan

  • ✓ Registration: GST Certificate, Udyam Aadhaar, Partnership Deed / MOA & AOA
  • ✓ Promoter KYC: PAN Card, Aadhaar Card, Passport photo of all Partners/Directors
  • ✓ Financials: Last 3 Years Audited Balance Sheet & Profit & Loss Account
  • ✓ Tax Returns: Last 3 Years Income Tax Returns (ITR) with Computation Sheets
  • ✓ Bank Statements: 12 Months Current Account Statement of all primary banks
  • ✓ Sanction Letters: Copy of existing loan/OD sanction letters, if any
