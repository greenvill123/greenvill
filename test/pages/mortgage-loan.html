Documentation

Required Documents For Mortgage Loan

  • ✓ Title Deed: Original Sale Deed / Gift Deed of Mortgaged Property
  • ✓ Parent Documents: Continuous chain of title documents for 30 Years
  • ✓ Encumbrance Certificate: EC from Sub-Registrar Office for 30 Years
  • ✓ Property Tax & Plan: Latest Municipal Property Tax Receipt & Approved Plan
  • ✓ KYC Proof: PAN Card, Aadhaar Card, Address Proof of all Property Co-Owners
  • ✓ Income Proof: 3 Years ITR / Salary Slips & 12 Months Bank Account Statement
