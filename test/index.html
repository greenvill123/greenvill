Questions & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Search or click any question below to find instant answers regarding our services.

Does Greenvill Associates charge any upfront service fee to customers? +
No. Greenvill Associates provides 100% free loan consultation and documentation assistance to our clients. We are compensated directly by our authorized banking partners as official Direct Selling Agents (DSA).
How fast can my loan be approved through Greenvill? +
With complete documentation, Personal Loans can be sanctioned within 24 hours, Business Loans in 48 hours, and Home/Mortgage Loans within 3 to 5 working days.
What is the minimum CIBIL score needed for loan approval? +
A CIBIL score of 700+ is preferred for lowest interest rates. However, if your CIBIL score is lower or you have no credit history, our experts match you with NBFC partners who accommodate low-credit profiles.
Can self-employed individuals get a Home Loan without standard ITR? +
Yes! We have specialized banking programs (Banking Surrogate & Turnover Programs) tailored specifically for self-employed business owners who do not possess traditional 3-year ITR files.
