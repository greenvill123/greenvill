What lenders tend to look at, and what is worth keeping ready before you apply.

Who it suits

  • Private limited companies

Documents usually asked for

  • Identity and address proof of promoters
  • Business registration and GST records
  • Income tax returns, usually two to three years
  • Audited financials, where applicable
  • Bank statements and a note on the purpose

Do I need collateral?

Not always. Lenders offer unsecured business loans up to certain limits and secured facilities beyond that. The amount, profile and lender decide the route.

My business is new. Can I still apply?

Some lenders work with newer businesses, but most ask for a track record of filings and bank activity. We can help you see which route fits.

What speeds up a business loan?

Clean financials, GST returns that match your bank statements, and a clear note on what the funds are for.
