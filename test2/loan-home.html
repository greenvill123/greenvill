How much can I borrow?

It depends on your income, existing obligations, the property’s value and the lender’s own policy. Lenders typically fund a percentage of the property cost, so plan for a down payment as well.

Can a home loan cover construction?

Yes, where the lender offers it. Funds are usually released in stages against the stage of work and the documents the lender asks for.

Fixed or floating rate?

Both exist, and each behaves differently when rates move. Compare the rate, the reset terms and the total cost — the lender’s sanction letter is what applies.
