Which loan suits a one-off need?

It depends on the amount, how quickly you need it and whether you can offer security. A secured loan is usually cheaper; an unsecured one is faster.

Can you transfer only part of a loan?

A balance transfer moves the whole outstanding loan to another lender. A top-up is the route for extra funds on the same loan.

Do you lend directly?

No. Greenvill Associates is a loan facilitation and consulting firm working as a Direct Selling Agent. The lender decides eligibility, rates and approval.
