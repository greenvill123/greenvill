How much can I borrow against my property?

Lenders lend a percentage of the property’s assessed value, after their own valuation. Income and existing obligations also matter.

Can I keep using the property?

Yes. A loan against property is a secured loan — you continue to own and use it. The lender holds a charge until it is repaid.

Who values the property?

The lender appoints the valuer, and that valuation drives the sanctioned amount, not an online estimate.
